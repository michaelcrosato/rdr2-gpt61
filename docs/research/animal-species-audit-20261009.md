# Animal species audit — 2026-10-09

This is an open source inventory proposal for Dust & Mercy, not an implementation or acceptance receipt. The accompanying [candidate data](animal-species-candidates-20261009.json) expands named children of the current animal-family parents without changing [the main ledger](../../content/source-coverage.json). Every proposed child has original-design and runtime status `unimplemented`. Existing generic hunt animals do not establish the distinct compendium, habitat, behavior, visual, custody, loot and progression obligations of any proposed child.

The inspected ledger has **83 animal-family parents, 16 legendary-animal rows, 15 fish rows and no animal-species child rows**. Those are local file counts, not a source completion percentage. The current research policy already requires individual species and variations; family parents alone cannot satisfy it. See [source research](../source-research.md#ecology-geography-and-cast).

## Access and evidence limits

Research used indexed excerpts of the designated [Animals](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Animals) and [Redemption 2 species](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Animals/Redemption_2_species) pages, plus the linked family articles cited below, on **2026-10-09**. Search reported robots restrictions and supplied indexed text. No direct request, alternate-host bypass, browser visit or retry of a restricted fetch was made. The displayed crawl labels were generally “3 months ago”; they are not publication dates or pinned revision identifiers. Exact revisions and complete pagination remain unverified.

The index describes varieties and places horses/fish in separate catalogs. Its headline number is not an accepted target: its table includes legendary entries already represented in the ledger and creatures whose individual articles expressly deny a compendium entry. Candidate data retains these disagreements rather than converting the headline into a completion gate. The broader animal/category pages also mix earlier games and Online. [Species index](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Animals/Redemption_2_species), [animal category](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Category%3AAnimals).

The JSON separates `story_compendium_candidates`, `story_non_compendium_candidates`, `special_variant_candidates`, `existing_legendary_joins` and `edition_flags`. Catalog-only names carry weaker evidence than individually sampled family descriptions. No source link is evidence that its complete article or every spawn condition has been read. No AI-generated Fandom “Quick Answers”, community thread, user blog, model-only file remnant or achievement count is used to settle a conflict.

The extracted proposal contains **162 compendium candidates, two separately classified non-compendium story creatures, four special-appearance/individual candidates, 16 existing legendary joins and five sampled Online flags**. Named candidates link 82 existing family parents; the Horse parent's separate breed/coat catalog remains pending. These local counts describe this artifact's classification, not an accepted inventory total. In particular, adding the two story exceptions to compendium candidates and legendary joins produces a different total from the source headline; the exceptions must remain visible until exact revision reconciliation.

## Missable and non-compendium cases

The three macaws and island booby need an original limited-area observation opportunity independent of the main completion checklist. Guarma's three ordinary boa varieties must remain distinct from the theatre performer's separate 10-foot red boa. The snake article excludes that performance animal from study/skinning/compendium, while ordinary boas appear in its profile gallery. Fer-de-Lance also has a mainland location; it must not be marked island-exclusive merely because it appears in the island section. [Parrot](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Parrot), [Snake](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Snake_%28RDR_2%29), [Guarma](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Guarma), [missable content](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Missable_Content_in_Redemption_2).

The lion article describes a unique mission creature and expressly excludes it from the compendium; its subspecies is uncertain. The table label “Masai Lion” is retained as an alias requiring reconciliation, not accepted zoology. The green sea turtle is likewise an existing story creature with a journal/study distinction rather than an animal-compendium child. Sources disagree on turtle skinning, so that action stays unresolved. These creatures remain in scope as their own story obligations. [Lion](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Lion), [missable content](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Missable_Content_in_Redemption_2), [cut-content discussion](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Dead_Redemption_2_Cut_Content).

Parakeet depletion is a persistent ecological consequence. The article's main Challenges section describes first-kill, later dwindling and final extinction states, while an automated quick-answer blurb gives a conflicting population total. Candidate data adopts no automatic finite stock from that blurb; the main-section boundaries still need revision and runtime confirmation. The species is omitted from two achievements, which does not remove the original ecological obligation. [Parakeet](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Parakeet).

## Edition and naming reconciliation

Online's Naturalist sampling, mission sightings and Gus garment economy are separately flagged. They cannot silently become story-mode animal children. The four sampled Online-only legendary examples and the Online camp dog breed are retained as classification work, not a complete Online inventory or authorization to narrow the brief. The existing story legendary rows are joins, not duplicated new species. A shared legendary name across editions still requires separate conditions. [The Naturalist](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/The_Naturalist_%28Red_Dead_Online%29), [legendary animals](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Legendary_Animals), [Dog](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Dog_%28RDR2%29).

The index spells Australian Shepherd differently from the dog article. It also shortens or varies copperhead, diamondback and raccoon labels. Candidate aliases preserve those disagreements. A bighorn “Ram” table grouping maps to the existing Bighorn parent, and the three female wild sheep also join the Sheep parent; no invented `animal-family-ram` ledger ID is created. `Crustaceans` and `Equine` table groupings map to the existing Crab/Crayfish and Mule/Donkey parents. Horse breeds, coats and edition variants remain a separate pending audit.

## Remaining acceptance work

Each candidate still needs an exact source revision and individual behavior, habitat/time/weather, availability, study/track/kill/skin-or-pluck/pickup eligibility, clean-kill/quality, finite body and mount/satchel custody, spoilage, crafting/sale/donation, lawful consequence, sound/rig/animation and save-persistence specification. Those are separate predicates; some legitimately do not apply. The proposed original design templates in the JSON are requirements for future work, not authored or implemented counterparts.

Source breadth remains open for linked mission-only creatures, mangy/diseased or exceptional appearances, complete breed/coat catalogs, edition changes and category pagination. A complete species list would still not prove those dimensions. Runtime acceptance requires actual original actors, earned observations and interactions, finite ownership and loss, native presentation, meaningful negative/save tests and public controls. None of those gates is cleared by this audit.
