# Rival contract 2 — wounded mare and recoverable rear load

This is original balancing for scenes 11 and 12 of `the-names-they-took.md`, not a verified source-game rule or complete-game acceptance claim. It applies to records declaring both `rival.contractVersion: 2` and `rival.transportVersion: 1`, with their complete transport record. Earlier version-4 histories lacking this feature declaration and record keep their former behavior, including genuine earlier contract-2 histories. They acquire no invented pressure, wound, check or drop events. A declared feature without its record, or an unsupported feature version, is invalid.

A real lower carbine impact takes the original living Skein from 100 to 55 health. At 65 health or below, Levi steps down through the existing collision-checked dismount action and continues his separately reachable foot route. He can be tackled and bound without killing or replacing the mare or first catching him with a lariat. The first actual nonlethal wound is linked to its projectile receipt, costs two honor and one captive trust, and does not heal either body. Killing Skein retains the existing death outcome and moral cost. Inez must still physically approach and lead the same surviving mare; Thimble comes to Inez if her completed lead leaves her just outside her own remount range. A wounded recovered mare follows at 115 units/second rather than the healthy 185.

The actual passenger attachment owns the burden. Its existing 0.86 pace factor gains a snow factor of 0.94 (0.98 in other weather), a fatigue factor of 0.84 below 25 horse stamina, and a loose-load factor of 0.92 below 35 percent strap condition. Motion uses measured horse displacement, rather than a possibly stale velocity field. Moving load costs an additional 4.5 stamina/second in snow or 3.5 in other weather, multiplied by 1.4 above 160 units/second. With the existing normal four-point recovery, an ordinary loaded snow ride loses a modest half point/second; stopped recovery remains available. The objective HUD exposes the actual strap and horse-stamina percentages, including coarse-pointer phone layouts.

After twelve actual moving seconds the player receives a weather/burden warning. Strap wear accumulates only during motion: 0.0018/second in snow or 0.0008 otherwise, plus 0.016/second above 160 units/second. At 45 percent a separate loose-strap warning appears. A hands-on breathing/strap check requires a stopped, dismounted player within 55 units, on the same ground level, with an unobstructed ground contact line. The actual check takes one second; an interrupted check earns no completion. A checked load can be resecured within thirty seconds through a separate 1.2-second stopped action. This repairs the same attachment's condition without granting a replacement person, rope, food or ammunition.

An unstrapped load, or a load at fifteen percent condition or less, becomes unsafe above 160 units/second. A warning starts at 0.25 unsafe seconds; after one second the same living bound Levi slips onto a collision-checked nearby ground position. His health is retained; the actual unsafe drop records one injury, trust loss and honor loss. He remains reachable through lift, load and strap. Historical load/strap flags cannot authorize a return with an absent or loose passenger, and return-route credit requires the actual strapped attachment. A voluntary set-down also preserves this same body. Separation over 250 units warns after three seconds and fails only after twenty actual exposed seconds, allowing a real turn-back and recovery.

The isolated verification uses the existing native full route, whose initial camp proximity fixtures are explicitly labelled; subsequent positive progression uses real movement, offered actions, elapsed work and projectiles. No positive test assigns a stage, actor health/life, attachment, pressure or outcome. The tests prove a nonlethal mounted body impact followed by independent foot tackle, the actual wounded-mare lead and camp return, unsafe drop/reload/holding of the same living captive, measured pace/stamina and timed check/resecure, interrupted-contact behavior, complete graph round-trips, and a separately labelled legacy-shape compatibility fixture. Browser HUD checks import an unchanged native-route artifact and are UI/Save/reload evidence, not a fresh public fourteen-stage route, native touch, physical controller or real iOS evidence. Fresh public routes and the broader source/full-game contract remain open.
