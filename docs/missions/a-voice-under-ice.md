## Original premise and cast

Silas Orr left the eviction convoy to carry the residents’ seized deeds to a northern signal shelter. He was intercepted at the old ice viaduct. His horse broke away from the guards, then was attacked near the frozen spillway; he climbed into a maintenance recess and has survived beneath a sheet of river ice. The voice comes through the viaduct’s stone drainage arches, making position and distance uncertain. Finding him requires reading physical traces and checking different echoes, rather than following a visible person marker to the summit.

Mara distrusts his decision to take documents while people needed his help. Inez remembers that he returned to a locked freight wagon for her during the eviction. Their disagreement is about whether useful evidence excused leaving the convoy, and whether Tomas has told the entire truth about the escape. Silas returns with an injury and a personal obligation; he does not immediately become a healthy shop or quest label.

- **Mara Vale:** scout; physically reads the trail, climbs, carries Silas and protects the loading operation. The mission develops her skepticism into a decision to act without first resolving blame.
- **Inez Pike:** sole traveling companion and active tracker/carrier. She rides a separate mount, examines sign, assists on the ledge and transports Silas while Mara diverts the pack. Her behavior must remain distinct from Tomas’s organizer role.
- **Silas Orr:** injured courier, frostbitten hands, torn left shoulder and permanent bite scars at the jaw. He can answer calls, cooperate with lifting and speak while being carried. He cannot climb or ride independently during this mission.
- **Elin Orr:** Silas’s older sister and a wool mender. Introduce her through a physical first meeting at the kiln. She asks for her brother, who is also responsible for her young son, **Fin Orr**. Neither is substituted by Ada; the Rusks retain their own crisis and care records.
- **Tomas Reed:** urges the search, prepares a supply plan and faces Inez’s account of the escape on return. He remains at camp with Gideon and, on the restrained branch, Pavel.
- **Moss Laird:** quarry hauler who receives Silas’s upper body at the camp doorway.
- **Vera Holl:** apprentice carpenter who supports Silas’s injured leg and clears a bed. Moss and Vera are two separate visible helpers with independent timing and dialogue.
- **Della Wren:** the residents’ bookkeeper; records the lost mount and the medical supply cost. She stands with Tomas after the rescue and raises the practical need to move east once the weather opens.
- **Copper:** Mara’s already owned mare; fear, feed, pack and bond persist from the opening.
- **Thimble:** Inez’s gray riding mare, newly introduced with ownership and temperament distinct from Copper and Tomas’s Moth.
- **Lark:** Silas’s chestnut courier horse. A recognizable saddle, torn mail pouch and body show what happened. Lark remains dead in every branch and replay cannot create an owned replacement.

These identities require their own art and later relationship authoring. Their appearance in this proposal does not close global source-character obligations or merge minor participants into an anonymous crowd.

## Ten playable stages

1. **Someone still waits.** Elin physically approaches Mara by the warmed stove. Fin has folded Silas’s scarf beside the unused bowl. Mara can ask whether Silas chose to leave; Elin explains the deeds and admits she does not know. Tomas urges a search; Inez steps forward rather than being assigned offscreen. Elin supplies a sealed rescue kit containing one warm ration, a dressing and a rope. This kit is authored emergency equipment, separate from the six recovered supplies, so a lawful opening branch cannot softlock the rescue. Briefing writes prerequisites and an expedition checkpoint. Retains B01–B02.

2. **A gun in the saddle.** Inez opens a locked rescue chest and loans a distinctive short two-barrel coach gun with two chambered shells and eight reserve shells to Copper’s weapon rack. Mara must physically approach Copper, open stored equipment, inspect the gun and secure its loaded case before departure; the revolver remains equipped for travel. Existing revolver state and ammunition remain separate. Inez saddles Thimble at the nearby rack; both mount normally. The gun remains community-owned until the completed rescue awards it to Mara. Every borrowed kit grant has a stable transaction ID and cannot repeat on talk, load or retry. Retains B03–B04.

3. **Up the spillway.** Ride alongside Inez, through the birch culvert and toward Ash camp. Her pace is controlled by slope, snow, Copper’s stamina and party separation. She dismounts at the camp and inspects embers; Mara inspects the bootprints and paper wrapper. The track evidence is younger than the snowfall and points toward the viaduct. A wrong side trail is recoverable through Inez’s call and the map’s last confirmed sign. Retains B05–B06 and B08.

4. **What the convoy hid.** Follow Silas’s track to the ford. Hoofprints stop at the water, so Mara must inspect the downstream ice scrape and locate the opposite-bank exit. Inez follows the last verified sign, never cuts straight through river/cliff collision. During the ride she recounts Tomas ordering a loaded brake wagon through the impound gate before everyone was clear; the wagon crushed porter Edda Quill, Neri was carried out and later died of exposure, and two people, Bren Saye and Oren Holt, remain missing. Mara questions her defense of Tomas. Dialogue triggers once at route landmarks and can pause when the player must listen to a call. The account is original, with future character obligations still open. Retains B07.

5. **An answer below the ice.** Inspect Lark’s saddle and wounds; Inez recognizes Silas’s mail pouch. A clear tutorial offers a controlled upward signal shot with the revolver. The shot consumes real ammunition and spawns a projectile toward open sky. Silas answers from the arches. The player may also shout again to resolve the echo, but cannot skip the first authored signal/reply event. One sealed emergency signal cartridge is in the kit if prior combat exhausted both the revolver and its reserve; the accepted signal action loads and fires this cartridge as one transaction, so it cannot be diverted to unrelated firing before the required signal. Inez identifies the foot path, both mounts hitch and a checkpoint records them alive at the shelf. Retains B09–B11.

6. **Hands on stone.** Equip the coach gun from Copper before leaving the shelf. Traverse two visible step-up ledges, brace across a narrow ice lip, crouch beneath the low stone-and-ice arch and secure the rope. Climbing uses actual height bands and stamina; the arch has real clearance that standing traversal cannot pass. The first climb teaches consuming a warm ration. If no personal food remains or Mara declines it until stamina is low, Inez offers a measured sip of warm molasses water from her own flask; accept consumes that one-use companion resource. Resting in shelter restores enough stamina to continue after the flask is spent. No input requires rapid button tapping, and an assisted single-press mode preserves timing/cost. Retains B11–B12.

7. **The courier’s weight.** Locate the correct recess from Silas’s replies, then reach him through the climb route. He explains that the documents fell into the drainage channel; the required rescue is not delayed by recovering them. Inspect breathing, apply the mission dressing, and lift him. His frostbite limits how long Mara can leave him exposed. Carrying changes gait, stamina and collision width. The low outbound arch cannot accommodate a carried adult, so Inez identifies the wider southern ramp: 1860,420 → 1810,490 → 1670,585 → 1530,665 → 1380,650. At its ledges, the player hands Silas across to Inez, climbs down, then physically receives him again. Lowering him on a marked rest pad is recoverable. At the upper sheltered pad, an optional secured-rest interaction enables the dispatch-case recovery described below. Tossing him over an edge or shooting either person fails explicitly. Retains B13.

8. **Clear the loading spur.** Three hungry timber wolves appear on the crest, with a visible warning before approach. Mara transfers Silas into Inez’s arms at the spur. Inez then carries him along a authored 100-unit route to Thimble; she must really arrive, lift him to the rear saddle and secure the rescue strap before mounting in front. Mara calls from the far side of the culvert to draw the pack away. Wolves initially assess, then select a target by proximity, noise and line of sight; the player’s diversion changes their target rather than merely setting a tutorial flag. One circles toward Inez, one tests Mara’s position and one hesitates until another attacks. Refuge rocks obstruct bites and bullets; a wolf may flee wounded. Killing every pack member is optional to ordinary rescue but required for the retained performance condition. Retains B14–B15.

9. **Ride the broken water.** Mount Copper and escort Inez, Thimble and her live passenger down the spillway. Two separate pairs pursue from opposite slopes, provisionally preserving the indexed encounter count pending direct source verification. Wolves actually descend their slopes and intercept the route; they do not spawn on top of Mara. On horseback, shoot with independently stored ammunition and real mounted aiming; slowing down creates safer shots while matching Inez’s pace. Riding too far ahead leaves her exposed; feedback calls begin before a timed abandonment failure. After the pursuit waves, Inez leads Thimble into the shallow return creek to break the scent trail. Copper must physically follow the authored creek nodes, then both mounts leave through the left-bank exit. In water, fresh hoofprint/scent samples stop accumulating; the wolves' tracker loses this trail instead of following an invisible straight line to the kiln. Completion records at least 180 continuous world units of creek travel by both mounts, with Silas still strapped to Thimble and the party within 120 units. A premature bank exit produces a readable call and a recoverable return to the creek, not instant failure. These are original simulation rules supporting the indexed water-route beat; exact source tracking mechanics remain unverified. Inez avoids unnecessary speed on ice and protects Silas. A collapsing sheet of ice visibly seals only the upper maintenance recess after the party passes, leaving the lower trail explorable. Retains B16 and the changed route in B20.

10. **A place beside the stove.** Cross the lower gate, ride physically into the kiln and dismount. Remove the secured passenger with Inez, then hand him to Moss and Vera, who move through the shelter entrance to a bed. Elin reprimands Silas for trusting documents more than his own life, then adjusts his blanket; Fin gives him the folded scarf. Tomas thanks Mara/Inez; Della records the lost horse and remaining medical needs. Their conversation identifies an eastbound route when the freeze lifts, while Mara questions how safe another company town can be. Write an original journal drawing and reflection. Silas gains a healing schedule, visible scars and a continuing relationship; he does not disappear at the completion screen. Retains B17–B19.

## Optional conditions, failure, checkpoints and replay

Keep the two source performance predicates independently visible: (1) kill every authored wolf and receive no wolf-bite damage, (2) at least 80% successful trigger actions against intended wolves after at least one shot. Separate wolf damage from cold/fall damage for the first predicate. A fled wolf remains a valid authored outcome and fails only the all-wolves predicate. Signal shots cannot inflate or spoil combat accuracy; accidental protected-person fire causes failure instead of counting as a hostile hit. Checkpoint retry makes the no-wolf-damage predicate ineligible for that attempt, matching the indexed source tip; full restart/replay supplies a fresh attempt. Verify exact source scoring rules before final acceptance.

Essential actors are Mara, Inez and Silas; essential riding mounts are Copper and Thimble. Death, deliberate assault on protected people, dropping Silas into lethal terrain, a mount’s death, sustained abandonment or failure to protect the loading operation produce a readable reason and checkpoint options. Do not count authored dead Lark as an essential mount failure. A missed climb or low stamina has local recovery before failure where safe. Missing the mandatory rack weapon stops departure with a readable retrieval prompt; repair corrupted/old loadouts through validation or migration rather than reproducing the source’s replay softlock.

Checkpoint locations: prepared kiln; confirmed Ash-camp trail; live mounts at the shelf before climbing; Silas stabilized before carry; loading spur before predator assessment; mounted descent before the first pursuit pair; refuge gate before the camp handoff. A checkpoint is tagged by mission ID, stage, stable checkpoint ID and label. Its data uses the same authoritative campaign/entity/region/weapon/inventory encoding as a save, excluding nested checkpoint data and replay backups to avoid recursion.
