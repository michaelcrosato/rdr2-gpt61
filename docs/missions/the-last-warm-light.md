## Experience and source evidence

The source opening joins a vulnerable community, companion-led travel in cold weather, deceptive negotiation, cover fighting, optional pursuit, interior supplies and clues, a close-range ambush, interrogation with alternatives, equipment recovery, mount calming/leading, a displaced recruit and a changed location. It rewards a mount and unlocks a missing-companion rescue. Optional performance conditions cover damage avoidance, supply gathering and shooting accuracy; failure protects essential companions and mounts. ## Original situation

Mara's community has escaped a railroad company's eviction convoy. A sudden freeze strands them at an abandoned lime kiln. Their cook, Neri Bell, dies of exposure before the stove can be lit. Organizer Tomas Reed sends Mara to secure fuel and blankets from Copperglass, a mountain signal station whose boiler chimney still smokes. Stable hand Inez Pike joins them to find the pack animals taken during the eviction.

Copperglass is occupied by company repossession guards under Ansel Voss. They are stripping the telegraph and plan to destroy the boiler house to prevent mountain residents using the line. Station operator Ada Rusk has locked herself in the relay room after guards injured her father. She will join the community only if they respect his rescue and preserve the message log naming another missing courier. Mara's actions determine whom the community shelters and what useful evidence survives.

## Original cast and locations

- **Mara Vale:** playable scout; practical wool coat, rifle sling, visible gloves; responds to Inez's impatience and Tomas's evasions through original dialogue.
- **Tomas Reed:** community organizer and negotiator; advances under the player's protection; he knows more about the eviction than he initially admits.
- **Inez Pike:** young stable hand and flanking companion; impulsive around frightened horses; an argument with Ada starts a trust beat rather than a copied scene.
- **Ada Rusk:** signal operator; frightened but technically capable; she can isolate the relay room from the steam fire once Mara earns her cooperation.
- **Gideon Rusk:** injured station keeper; his survival becomes a camp-care obligation and Ada's lasting relationship condition.
- **Ansel Voss:** named guard supervisor with a side-exit retreat and later freight-inspection role if he escapes.
- **Pavel Dune:** cornered guard in the coal store; ambush participant and interrogation subject with later consequences for release, restraint or killing.
- **Copper:** frightened dun pack mare taken from the eviction convoy; independent calming and ownership state.
- **Lime kiln refuge:** shelter, communal stove, mourning scene and return checkpoint.
- **Copperglass station:** winding approach trail, boiler yard, stone culvert cover, relay building interior, coal store loft, animal pen and service tunnel.

## Playable stages and state transitions

1. **Find heat.** The opening scene establishes Neri's death and the communal stove's empty fuel box. Mara must put on the provided coat, speak with Tomas and take the starter lantern. Inez asks that the player look for Copper. The player receives a visible primary objective and an accessible movement/interaction tutorial. Transition records the camp's cold emergency and the named lost courier, Silas Orr.
2. **Follow the wire.** Ride with Tomas through a narrow switchback while maintaining companion proximity. Inez is found inspecting a severed telegraph cable and joins the party. The route teaches pace matching, camera movement, stopping and dismounting. Wind and the red station lamp remain readable landmarks; a lost player receives a companion call and map trace.
3. **A company signature.** Hitch the traveling mounts outside rifle range. Tomas poses as a company messenger asking to inspect the boiler. Mara chooses culvert cover or the timber winch; both have working projectile obstruction. Inez finds an eviction tag on Copper's saddle and warns Mara. Voss identifies Tomas's community token and orders the guards to seize him. The resulting danger follows this original discovery, with visible threat before firing starts.
4. **Hold the boiler yard.** Fight three distinct roles: a guard beside the pressure gauge, a flanker using the coal chute and Voss at the relay doorway. Tomas withdraws between cover nodes while Inez protects the pen. Voss flees through the service tunnel after two guards fall. Pursuing him is optional and delays supply gathering; an escaped Voss later appears at a freight checkpoint. Companion damage, enemy routes, ammunition and obstruction operate as actual simulation state.
5. **What the station kept.** Search the relay building for six separate practical supplies: blankets, lamp oil, oat sack, bandages, canned broth and dry kindling. The optional logbook clue records a scheduled courier interception. An overturned medical chair and Gideon's torn sleeve establish the Rusks' conflict through original set dressing. Items enter inventory once and can be consumed or delivered; walking near a prop cannot complete collection.
6. **The coal-store bargain.** Pavel drops from a storage platform and disarms Mara. The short melee tutorial supports block, shove and restraint. Recover the dropped weapon and community token after subduing him. An interrogation reveals the pressure fuse and a route to Silas. Choose **release**, **bind for later judgment**, or **kill**. Each writes a distinct choice: release permits a later warning, restraint adds a guarded camp responsibility, killing changes Ada's trust and Tomas's dialogue. The player receives the necessary rescue clue regardless of branch to avoid a progression dead end.
7. **A name the mare knows.** Copper panics in the animal pen. Holster the weapon, approach slowly, speak, calm and pat before leading her to the hitch. Rushing or firing increases fear and can knock Mara back without instantly failing the mission. Correct recovery remains available. Identifying Inez's old rope halter confirms ownership history; Inez explicitly assigns Copper to Mara for the community's supply work. Riding, feeding and pack storage unlock after safe return.
8. **Keep the relay alive.** A remaining guard lights the demolition fuse; steam fire cuts off the main entrance. Investigate Ada's alarm, identify oneself through the speaking tube and choose a promise: preserve the message log or carry Gideon first. Both rescue them, but the order affects supplies and Ada's trust. The player opens the pressure relief valve, escorts Ada and carries Gideon along the stone service walkway. Ada shuts off the relay circuit after crossing. Destruction permanently changes the boiler wing; the relay remains damaged but usable in later content. A failed carry or route attempt restarts this checkpoint with both people alive and world objects restored.
9. **A light at the kiln.** Return with the Rusks, Copper and actual collected supplies. Deposit essentials in the stove store; inventory transfers exactly once. Ada joins the camp, Gideon needs care, Neri's empty place receives an original quiet acknowledgement, and the camp's next objective is Silas's rescue. Tomas and Inez react to the Pavel/Voss choices and rescued materials. The station's destroyed boiler, recovered log and resident departures persist in free roam.

## Availability, rewards and dependencies

This is the first playable campaign mission and cannot be skipped in a new story. The route teaches only the verbs required here; later hunting, fishing and advanced targeting retain their own missions. Successful return unlocks Copper's ownership, basic horse care/storage, camp supplies, Ada's relationship record and `snowbound-a-voice-under-ice`, the separate missing-courier rescue counterpart. That next mission is an authoring obligation and is not implemented by this specification.

Rewards are the actual recovered supplies and mare rather than an invented large cash payment. The camp consumes the deposited broth/kindling according to the survival system. The optional log preserves evidence for a later railroad-hearing branch. Losing it does not erase Silas's essential rescue lead. Moral reputation, companion trust and witness/law state are separate; this mission changes trust even if no public witness reports a crime.

Optional performance records are three separate predicates: avoid combat injury in the yard, collect all six essential supply objects, and maintain at least 80% accuracy against intended hostile targets after firing at least one shot. They do not gate campaign progress and must use real damage/item/projectile evidence.

## Failure, checkpoints and replay

Checkpoints occur before the station approach, yard combat, coal-store ambush and relay rescue. Player death, an essential companion's death, deliberate attacks on protected rescue participants or abandoning the rescue area during the active crisis cause a readable failure. The player can retry the latest checkpoint or restart the mission. Fear, missed shots, optional supplies and Voss's escape have recoverable or authored consequences.

Checkpoint restoration must include actors, injuries, equipment, picked-up objects, mounts, guards, fire/fuse state and mission-local timers. It must prevent repeated supply or ownership grants. A versioned save can suspend at each safe checkpoint; reloading preserves the selected branch and world state. Mission replay uses a separate session snapshot so replaying cannot duplicate items, overwrite canonical choices or resurrect people in the permanent world.
