## Original situation and dramatic function

Bellwether Works once washed quarry stone for a cooperative. Its radial wagon traverser, sloping sled gallery and covered weighhouse now serve the Claimants, a foreclosure crew that confiscates equipment under dubious railroad warrants. Their commander, Rafe Calder, plans to divert a bond-and-deed train and sell the same families' land twice. Tomas Reed once trusted Calder to negotiate a quarry settlement; Calder used the signatories to identify homes for seizure. Tomas's resentment makes a necessary evidence search look dangerously like revenge.

Mara must help a real expedition obtain the seizure route and powder equipment, defeat a positioned crew and returning patrols, and bring home an adult who knows how its warrants were made. The battle takes place across a functioning wagon yard and forest approaches, rather than the opening's boiler station. The fugitive is neither Pavel Dune nor Silas Orr. The operation ends with a human obligation that cannot be reduced to a captured-item counter.

The party's success gives Tomas confidence that he can control the railroad's next move. Della's account and the captive's uncertain testimony question that confidence. A later train mission must own its own preparation, boarding, rescue, conflict and aftermath. This mission supplies its actual prerequisites; it does not implement that mission by announcing a train job.

**Levi Senn**, 24, is a farrier's assistant compelled to copy debt warrants for the Claimants after Calder bought his family's arrears. He is observant about horses and distances, evasive about signatures and terrified that every armed group will treat him as a uniform. His mount **Skein**, a pale bay mare with a split ear, has separate health, fear, tack and ownership. Levi can flee, be bound, carried, stowed, questioned and held; he never aliases Pavel, a bounty target, an inventory stack or a generic rescued patient. His later trust, horse work, testimony, companion activities and eventual danger remain separate future authoring obligations.

Rafe Calder rides away during reconnaissance on his own mount, **Grout**. Neither is an invulnerable actor left within firing reach. If the player exposes the party during that phase, the reconnaissance attempt fails and has a real checkpoint recovery. His escape is visible movement along an outgoing road with legitimate cover and distance.

If Pavel was released in the opening, his existing body/identity may return as a Claimant shed guard, with a private exchange about his interrogation and a recognition response to Mara. If Pavel is held at the kiln, he remains there. If he died, a different guard fills the ordinary combat role without inheriting his identity or personal lines. This condition preserves three existing outcomes rather than redefining all spared outcomes as release.

## Geography, staging and battle populations

Author a new roughly 3,000 × 2,400-unit region, `bellwether-works`, with a south snow gate, branched cart trail, west observation ridge, steep screened foot descent, east horse ramp, covered wagon traverser, weighhouse, powder magazine, forest return paths and a frozen pulley creek. These dimensions are production proposals, not tested geometry. World units, elevations, doorways and cover must be authored together before implementation.

The wagon traverser is a circular rail platform with real blocking machinery and three entrances. The gallery provides elevated sight lines and a low protected crossing. The weighhouse has an accessible ground doorway and a screened loft window. The magazine has two physical access points, shelving and a separate charge work surface. At least two mount paths must remain traversable after fighting. Snow walls, machinery, trunks and roof beams must appear where collision and projectile obstruction exist.

Original initial balancing uses thirteen yard defenders, plus the conditional Pavel identity when released, and twelve separately identified returning patrol members. The patrol is an existing off-yard population with route, hearing and timing; looting cannot create enemies from nothing. Tower watchers, pushing riflemen, low-cover defenders, flankers, runners and patrol scouts need different behaviors. The return attack arrives from two wooded approaches with a later reserve group. This scale retains a group battle and enough real individual opportunities for the precision objective; final balance must preserve breadth rather than replacing the battle with three guards.

Allies move, observe, fire, reload, take cover, retreat and suffer real damage. Suppression can help Mara find an angle; it cannot make every ally shot cosmetically harmless or every enemy an immortal target waiting for the player. Finite enemies may retreat or surrender under authored conditions. Their life, inventory, weapons and presence persist. Neutralization, player kill, ally kill, disarm, surrender and escape are different events.

## Connected systems and consequential alternatives

**Equipment:** the original Tern is a seven-round lever repeater with its own cartridge type, reload cadence, recoil, spread, range, muzzle height and rack identity. Provisional issue is seven loaded cartridges plus forty-two reserve, granted once. Lariat, sightglass, cap tin, charge crate, route diagram and seizure list are separately identified objects. Quest cargo stays under actual custody; it is not unlimited ammunition. The charge crate is sealed for the later plan, with inspect/carry/storage verbs and truthful collision/hazard treatment. If an authored explosion destroys essential charges before securing them, use a stated failure/recovery path rather than recreating them on collection.

**Focus:** the operation introduces automatic marks through actual reticle sweep over visible target geometry. Store target ID, local hit point, chronological order, weapon and activation ID; cap the queue to loaded rounds. Firing attempts each marked shot through present cover and range. Misses, dead/moving targets, empty ammunition, cancelled marks and protected bodies remain real outcomes. Damage continues in slowed simulation time. Teach with pausable readable instruction and accessible control alternatives. Manual individual marking, continued free fire and fatal/critical highlight upgrades remain locked to later owners. A rival-first hunt can use this earned ability; a hunt-first route cannot acquire it by reading this specification.

**Disarm variation:** author a specific yard opportunity in which a disarmed guard grapples Bastian and takes his engraved revolver. Recovering the actual dropped/stolen weapon can return it or establish a disputed player claim, with ownership and relationship consequences. No duplicate revolver, repeated immortal knife animation or unexplained respawn is allowed. The exact source anecdote remains unverified and the relevant unique-weapon requirement remains open; this original encounter supplies a functional variation of unusual acquisition without adopting a bug as a ruleset.

**Tactics and search:** first-shot and second-battle choices are independent. Advancing cannot finish the patrol merely by passing a waypoint. Holding cannot permanently hide a reserve behind an unloaded map. Bodies retain finite pockets and spent weapon ammunition; ally searches have their own named transfers. A personal letter records a former quarry worker's role, making Tomas's claim that every opponent is interchangeable visibly questionable.

**Captive treatment:** Levi's identity, bound state, wounds, hunger, trust, statements and holding time persist independently. A compassionate outcome still supplies a guarded, disputed holding and future testimony relationship, not an instant completed redemption arc. Coercive answers may be unreliable; the physical diagram is still necessary. Food and blankets consume real available stock, with explicit no-stock alternatives; deprivation does not award imaginary confiscated food. Mara can object to Bastian, check Levi's breathing and visit later. The protagonist cannot kill the required captive and continue with a substitute.

**Skein:** retain Levi's mount as a real animal if safely recovered; Inez can lead her after securing the fugitive. Harm changes health/death, trust and moral state. A later chapter must use this actual identity or a lawfully acquired replacement. The source's apparent later horse continuity after a fatal chase outcome remains an audit question, not permission to resurrect animals. Copper death/abandonment and party harm retain their protected failure contracts.

**Order and camp life:** Rescue completion opens Hunt and this operation independently. Completion of both is the next operation's necessary gate; its equipment/data requirements also need satisfaction. Rival-first adds Levi's two distinct holding-room visits before the hunt and changes Juno's mouths-to-feed/return account using actual facts. Hunt-first retains earlier camp conversations until the rival operation completes. Separate fire-side leadership doubt, bereavement/care, packing reassurance, reading/faith and captive visits need authored original equivalents and individual evidence; this document does not close the full camp-event inventory. Allow a visible final-mission readiness pause so players can inspect outstanding camp obligations, with that original timing improvement recorded rather than pretending it is the source trigger.

Keep five independent optional performance records. These are authoring balance values, not asserted source numbers.
