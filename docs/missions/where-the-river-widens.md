## Original premise

The train's disputed deeds have survived, but the kiln has become a recognizable address. People need water, work and room for an infirmary before Morrow's investigators can turn a refuge into an enclosure. Della has located **Longbank Commons**, a seasonal carting terrace above the **Rillwater River**, within reach of the market town of **Dustwater**. The terrace belongs to the **Whitebank river cooperative** under a contested communal-use agreement. Staying there requires permission and conduct; recovering the quarry households' deeds does not confer ownership of another community's land.

Tomas calls the move a chance to begin again. Della insists that a different address cannot erase an account. Mara drives the work wagon while Della navigates; Juno joins their rear bench after the roadside repair. The family and care wagon travels separately. A broken retaining ring releases a real wheel at the ford exit, exposing the difference between a convoy of people and a collection of cargo. Later, three Whitebank riders watch the repair from their own boundary path. The community reaches a usable home only by unloading, arranging care and agreeing who does the everyday work.

The recovered securities remain unsold. A new credit broker offers to turn that fact into a claim on future wages. Food and shelter improve the day without resolving the property case, captive testimony, strained relationships or industrial conflict. The move opens a broader world and introduces further people whose entire stories still require authoring.
