# Dust & Mercy — source research and coverage ledger

The requested end state is a finished, original game covering variations of every mission, battle, side quest, character, item, job, progression system, creature and other described beat in the supplied RDR2 wiki. A vertical slice is an intermediate quality proof. It does not clear that goal.

This research establishes an expandable source inventory. It does **not** establish complete source extraction, complete design, or full gameplay coverage. The machine-readable record is [content/source-coverage.json](../content/source-coverage.json); entries remain `unimplemented` until source-specific code and runtime are independently inspected. The opening, northern rescue, companion hunt and rival operation now record `in_progress` implementation evidence. None is accepted; full source/presentation/input and later-dependency acceptance remains open. A newly authored specification is design evidence, not implemented gameplay.

## Evidence and source access

The user supplied [Red Dead Redemption 2](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Dead_Redemption_2). Direct web access returned HTTP 402 and subsequent direct paths were restricted by robots rules. Research therefore used indexed excerpts of the same wiki through web search on 2026-10-08. Individual campaign and stranger article links below were found in indexed results. This fallback permits named-entry confirmation and sampled narrative research; it does not prove that all live article sections, linked pages, revision changes or category pagination have been read.

No numerical wiki total is accepted as the final content target. Articles combine numbered parts differently, mix honor tasks with story missions, include cinematics, and sometimes allow a mission across chapter boundaries. The catalog itself has both grouped strands and separately numbered parts. Each row below identifies a source obligation, rather than promising that the row is one separately playable mission. The [mission catalog](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Missions_in_Redemption_2) and [mission category](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Category%3ARedemption_II_Missions) require reconciliation against individual articles before the inventory can be sealed.

The source's built-in completion checklist is weaker than this brief: many optional activities, collection entries and encounters are outside that checklist. Passing a “100%” counter therefore cannot prove this requested full scope. See [100% Completion](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/100%25_Completion_%28RDR2%29).

## Campaign inventory

All chapter headings are catalog groupings, not a dependency graph. Proposed Dust & Mercy chapter names identify authoring work to come; they are not implemented content. The original story centers Mara Vale and a community threatened by a railroad syndicate. Original variations must retain the distinct gameplay and dramatic function of each obligation while authoring new events, people, dialogue, places, objectives and outcomes.

## Activities, companions and ambient life

The [activity inventory](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Activities_in_Redemption_2) confirms bounty work, camp/ranch chores, fishing, horse taming, hunting, treasure hunts, blackjack, dominoes, finger-filleting, races, poker and shooting contests. Each needs a complete ruleset, opponents or targets, rewards, persistence and authored contexts.

The ledger separately records the indexed companion activities at the early, middle and urban camps: games, paired hunts/fishing, homestead and coach robberies, rustling, and the edition-specific bank activity. These require their own conversations, routes, conflicts and conclusions; they cannot all be represented by one generic activity button. [Companion activity catalog](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Missions_in_Redemption_2), [camp structure](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Camps).

Companion requests are individual relationships and triggers. The ledger includes the indexed money, grooming, herb, feather, keepsake, book, tool, liquor, animal and remedy requests, but exact quantities, rewards and timing still need validation. [Errand Boy](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Errand_Boy), [missable-content guide](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Missable_Content_in_Redemption_2).

[Random encounters](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Random_Encounters_in_Red_Dead_Redemption_2) and [camp events](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/Camp_Events) expand the target beyond visible quest icons. The latter article is marked under construction. Named ambient figures, territorial ambushes, crimes, rescues, repeat meetings, celebrations, arguments and time-dependent conversations require individual records. Neither category is exhaustively itemized yet.

- **inventory-seal:** The requested wiki and linked guide scope has not been fully traversed or pinned to article revisions. Source inventories remain open; row totals are not final source totals.
- **mission-beats:** Campaign titles are indexed; five opening requirements now have sampled source-to-original scene specifications, four with in-progress runtime. Full mission-by-mission beats, battles, objectives, optional branches, companion roles, failure rules, cinematic aftermath, unlock edges and medal objectives remain unsealed and require source reconciliation, implementation and acceptance.
- **stranger-parts:** Each stranger strand needs its individual numbered meetings, conditional branches, availability windows, collectibles, rewards and aftermath audited. Apparent part counts vary with framing scenes.
- **special-editions:** Special/Ultimate edition missions, unlocks and items need explicit original equivalents. Online-only and cut material must be tagged separately instead of silently included or omitted.
- **ambient-events:** The random encounter index is not exhaustively itemized; every named event, sequence, region, time window, repeat occurrence and lasting consequence remains in scope.
- **camp-events:** Camp conversations, celebrations, activities, requests, rewards, songs, arguments and schedules are not exhaustively decomposed. The wiki camp-event page itself is under construction.
- **challenges-ranks:** All 90 rank conditions have individual indexed records. Detailed counter/reset audits, rewards, equipment unlocks, original designs and implementation remain open; the Horseman rank-2 reward discrepancy needs resolution.
- **bounties-robberies:** Thirteen poster jobs and eight mission-attached captures have individual indexed records. Catalog reconciliation, target beats, delivery branches, sheriff aftermath, edition history, home robberies, shop side businesses, coach tips and hideout variants remain open.
- **wildlife-species:** Animal-family parents are not a full species inventory. Every compendium species, breed/coat, legendary variant, habitat, study/track/kill/skin state, animation and loot rule must be expanded.
- **items-recipes:** All provisions, tonics, ingredients, ammunition variants, bait, lures, recipes, pamphlets, tools, equipment, clothing, hats, outfits, satchels, documents, cards, valuables, talismans, trinkets and unique rewards are not individually inventoried.
- **geography:** Major locations are inventoried; every smaller locality, interior, business, landmark, shack, grave, encounter area and point of interest remains in scope.
- **cast:** The prominent cast is inventoried; stranger patrons, debtors, bounty targets, gang members, merchants, service staff, lawmen, named ambient figures and mentioned/deceased personalities require separate expansion.
- **progression:** The entire campaign and post-story state graph, moral outcomes, attribute levels, focus abilities, equipment perks, economies and completion trackers require implemented-and-played evidence.
- **implementation-audit:** The opening, northern rescue and companion hunt supply code and bounded independent state/player-control/presentation evidence, recorded as in progress. None is accepted: full source, presentation, input, branch and later-dependency gates remain open. Other entries default to unimplemented until their own inspected runtime evidence exists; specifications alone do not change implementation status.
- **presentation-polish:** Original assets, animations, environmental and musical direction, sound, readable UI, input parity, camera work, performance, accessibility and cross-browser/mobile verification remain completion gates.
- **final-presentation:** The requested final boardroom report requires evidence from the finished game: approach, design choices, effort allocation, engine assessment, technology rationale, validation and full coverage reconciliation.

Research and implementation progress are independent. To close a source requirement, record the specific original counterpart, code/content paths, authored beats or rules, asset/animation identifiers, prerequisites and outcomes, then supply successful runtime evidence covering it. Parent taxonomy records close only when every enumerated child closes and the source inventory is sealed.

A manifest entry, generated title, chapter-select button, placeholder actor, one reusable interaction, automated count, or green smoke test cannot alone prove content or gameplay completion. Acceptance must include authored variety, functional outcomes and intended presentation quality. Any automated coverage report must show pending inventory expansion as an open gate and must never infer a full completion percentage from the current row count.
